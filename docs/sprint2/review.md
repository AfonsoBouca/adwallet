## Demo Summary
Demonstrated working features:
- Watch ads and answer questionnaires (working, reward added to wallet)
- Advertiser upload form (validation, confirmation, pending state)
- Rate ads with star system (optional, does not block reward)
- Earnings history (list, empty state, total)
- Donation confirmation with error handling (insufficient balance handled)

## Product Feedback
- Feedback 1: The advertiser upload form validation is good, but users need clearer error messages when the reward is out of bounds.
- Feedback 2: The donation history is functional, but sorting by charity in addition to date would be a nice enhancement.
- Feedback 3: The notification preferences work locally, but integration with a real push notification service will be needed for production.
